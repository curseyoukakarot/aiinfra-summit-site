[ General Information ]

What is AI INFRA Summit 2025?

AI INFRA SUMMIT 2025 is an industry-leading event that brings together AI developers, enterprise leaders, venture capitalists, and technology experts to explore the future of AI infrastructure. We dive deep into everything from foundational hardware and edge devices to advanced cloud APIs—linking the practical “how” of applied AI with the strategic “why” of robust infrastructure.

When and where will the event take place?

The summit is scheduled for May 02 in 2025. It will be held in Microsoft Mountain View and will also offer virtual attendance options to accommodate a global audience. Please refer to our registration page for complete details.

Is the event in-person, virtual, or hybrid?

AI INFRA SUMMIT 2025 is designed as a hybrid event, allowing you to attend in-person at our venue or participate virtually. This flexible format ensures that everyone can benefit from the summit, no matter where they are located.

[ Audience & Participation ]

Who should attend the summit?

Whether you’re an AI developer, an enterprise leader, a venture capitalist, or an industry expert, this event is designed for anyone interested in the convergence of AI innovation and scalable infrastructure. It’s a unique opportunity to network, gain insights, and explore the future of technology.

How can I register for the event?

Registration is easy! Simply click the “Register Now” button on our landing page to secure your spot. Detailed ticketing information, including pricing and any early bird offers, is available on the registration section of our website.

[ Content & Speakers ]

What topics and themes will be covered?

The summit will explore a range of topics, including:

  • The rapid evolution of AI and its impact on various industries
  • The critical role of robust, scalable infrastructure—from edge devices to hyperscale data centers
  • Ecosystem synergy and collaborative strategies between AI and infrastructure providers
  • Strategic decisions and innovative technologies driving next-generation deployments

For a full agenda and list of sessions, please see the “Speakers & Agenda” section.

Our summit features a dynamic lineup of industry visionaries, top technology leaders, and subject matter experts. For detailed information on our speakers and session topics, please visit the “Speakers & Agenda” page on our website.

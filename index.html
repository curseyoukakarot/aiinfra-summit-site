What to expect on the day

One day, engineered end-to-end: a tight 15-minute main-stage cadence, curated breakouts, live demos, 1:1 deal-flow, giveaways, and hallway time that actually compounds. MC’d, timed, zero filler — then a happy hour and afterparty where the real deals get done.

How Ignite curates

Every summit is programmed from a standing start. No recycled panels, no pay-to-play keynotes: sessions are sourced from what operators are shipping and what capital is chasing right now, then pressure-tested with the community until the agenda reads like a map of the market.
